# 06-EXTERNAL-INPUTS — external inputs for the class d = 2 (copies of artifacts from sibling projects)

The class d = 2 in the article relies on the results of two sibling (not yet published) projects. To make the manuscript and the package self-contained, **exactly those artifacts** from which the numbers of the d = 2 row are taken (code, certificates, reports, JSON) are copied here, preserving their internal names. Nothing in them was recomputed here; their rigor status is given as established in the source projects.
